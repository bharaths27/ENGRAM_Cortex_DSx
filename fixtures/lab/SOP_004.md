# SOP 004: Acid rinse for Sample B

This is a made up demo document for the ENGRAM Cortex hackathon demo.

## Scope
This procedure covers moving Sample B from storage to the bench for the acid rinse step.

## PPE
Safety goggles must be at the bench before handling Sample B. Gloves are worn at all times.

## Steps
1. Bring the safety goggles to the bench.
2. Bring Sample B from shelf A to the bench.
3. Wait for the student to confirm before the rinse starts.

## Storage
Sample A and Sample B are stored on shelf A. Goggles are kept at the PPE station.
